# Research objective and sequence

The user's objective is a coherent mechanistic account of complete fly navigational behaviors, assembled from papers that each characterize only part of the system. A motivating question is: if a fly remembers something, how can that memory guide it from its current situation to the relevant destination?

## Literature first

Read the relevant primary literature completely, including Methods, figures, Extended Data and supplements. Each paper has its own folder and notes.md. Record exact coverage and asset access gaps in reading-log.md; acquisition and abstract reading are not completed review. Start from the existing 21-paper corpus and follow necessary references; do not equate that initial corpus with every relevant paper in the field.

Use the depth and evidence distinctions in the Lyu notes as the standard. For each paper identify inputs, outputs, mathematical computation, experimental behavior, represented variables, reference frames, timescales, model assumptions, causal evidence, and connections to neighboring circuits. Distinguish an odor association, a persistent goal bearing, a displacement vector, a remembered place, and a route. Do not assume that a fly uses one particular spatial strategy merely because it can explain a trajectory.

Maintain a cross-paper synthesis organized by complete behaviors. Separate demonstrated links, model-supported links, and missing links; reconcile naming and differences between preparations and connectomes. Avoid inventing a single serial pathway where parallel control or behavioral switching may be needed.

## Structure and function after the literature synthesis

Use connectomics and, where informative, simulations to investigate candidate functions of unexplained cells and circuits. Prioritize questions revealed by the synthesis rather than exhaustively testing every parameter or open question. Existing preliminary graph extraction is infrastructure, not functional discovery.

Any proposed new function must be labeled a hypothesis, with its anatomical support, physiological constraints, alternative explanations and relevant behavioral contribution stated. A simulation demonstrating sufficiency is not identification of the actual biological mechanism. No promise of discovering a unique function from connectivity alone.
