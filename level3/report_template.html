Family tree

Which run started from which. An arrow from a parent to a child means the child began from the parent's weights (a warm start) and changed the one knob written on the arrow. Runs with no arrow in started from random numbers. Colour is the best 20-second score: greener is better.

Journeys, overlaid

The training score of every selected run on one chart. Tick the runs to show. The solid line is the running score; the shaded band under each is the drift gap, the space between the frozen brain's score with F allowed and with F held at zero. A wide band means the memory is in F.

Iteration. One round of training: run a batch of trips, measure, nudge every slow strength a little. Stage. The curriculum: 10-second wanders first, then 20, then 30. The dotted verticals mark a run moving up a stage.

Words

Neuron a dot holding one number, its activity. Connection an arrow between two neurons carrying a strength. Slow strength fixed by training. Fast strength (F) changes while the brain runs; the tally marks. A how much each connection's F counts. Gate a 0-to-1 number the brain outputs: write lets F grow, erase makes it shrink. Score mean closest approach to food on the return, lower is better. Iteration one training round. Checkpoint a saved copy of the brain. Frozen training off. Warm start beginning a run from another run's saved weights. Correlation a number from −1 to +1 for how much two things rise and fall together. Saturated a fast strength stuck at its ceiling, unable to record more.
