# Round 5 charter (29 Sept, written before any run; 45-minute budget)

Question: is FW easy to train because its memory is "one knob away", and do the rivals fail at HOLDING the way home or at READING it?

A. Replication of round 4 with 5 random starts per group (seeds 0-4) and the step-size decay (cosine) that keeps FW stable. 10 s stage, 1,200 iterations, step 1e-3, batch 32, 2 s at food, erase charge 0.05. Groups: FW; FW with write strength starting at 0 (empty board); TWIN; TWIN with a head start (16 slow heading counters). Predictions: FW-empty learns as often as FW (within 1 start of 5) (65%); TWIN learns on 0 of 5 (80%); TWIN-HS learns on at most 2 of 5 (60%). "Learns" = frozen 10 s exam below the never-steers line (2.59) by at least 0.2.

B. Hold versus read, after training, every network: (1) HOLD = R^2 of a straight-line readout of the home vector from the memory at the turn (board for FW, activity for twins), fitted on 300 flies and scored on 300; (2) READ = correlation between the network's own turn command on the first 3 s of the return and the ideal turn toward home, sin(direction home - heading). Prediction: TWIN-HS spinners HOLD (R^2 > 0.5) but do not READ (corr < 0.2), i.e. the rivals' bottleneck is reading, which FW's board does by multiplying with the current activity (the query). 55%.

## Result (15:11)
Exam at 10 s (learned = below 2.39): FW 1.21, 2.75, 1.99, 2.37, 2.52 (3 of 5); FW with an empty board 1.39, 2.21, 2.77, 2.74, 1.41 (3 of 5; write strength past 0.02 by iteration 30-40 in all five); TWIN 2.74-2.81 (0 of 5); TWIN-HS 2.81, 1.57, 1.61, 2.78, 2.78 (2 of 5). Predictions A: all three held. Prediction B (hold but not read): held in substance: every failed network still held the way home (R^2 0.45-0.88), but the "read" measure planned here (turn/ideal-turn correlation) proved uninformative on reference networks (a taught twin that homes scored -0.02), so it was replaced before the round-5 networks were analysed by (i) heading error, also too noisy, and then (ii) a readout of the ideal turn from the activity. On untrained networks that readout is equal for FW (0.41-0.53) and the head-start twin (0.45-0.55), which rules out "FW's advantage is a built-in multiplication for reading".
